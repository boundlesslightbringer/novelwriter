```
Recommended next POV: Vara.

Reasoning: Caradhen's chapter (Ch. 14) ended on a cliffhanger — Vara's unanswered question is hanging in the scene. However, cutting away is the correct call here: the reader knows Vara asked something Caradhen cannot easily answer; dwelling in his perspective will dissipate the tension rather than build it. Vara's POV is the right cut because: (1) the reader does not yet know what Vara is actually assessing — her internal state has not been rendered; (2) Vara's assessment brief is the mechanism through which Aldric's contingency becomes relevant; (3) her courier has already departed, which means her chapter has a natural structural question: what exactly did she send?

Simultaneous events to account for:
  Aldric's contingency instruction (Day 46): This was issued while Caradhen's chapter was occurring. Vara does not know about it — she is at the passes, the instruction was issued at the capital, and no communication has reached her. The next chapter must not give Vara knowledge of this event. It is, however, running in Vara's chapter's temporal background: the reader knows it exists; Vara does not. This is active dramatic irony and should be preserved.
  Vara's courier: The courier departed Day 45. The next chapter is set on Day 47–48. The courier is en route but has not arrived. No information has been delivered yet. This is consistent and requires no intervention.

Rotation pressure flags:
  Vara — Medium/High: Her storyline has direct momentum; the assessment brief is the next structural beat.
  Aldric — High: His contingency instruction has been issued but not shown from his perspective. His storyline is accumulating unrevealed consequence. He needs a chapter within the next 2–3 to show what he has actually authorised and why.
  Caradhen — Low: His cliffhanger is best left unresolved for at least one chapter. His pressure will build in his absence.
  Sethric — Low: Dormant thread; no pressure.

Cliffhanger handling: Cut away. Reasoning above.

POV proliferation check: Current active POVs: 3 (Vara, Aldric, Caradhen). No new POV proposed. Current rotation period is manageable — each character appears roughly every 2–3 chapters. No structural pressure.
```
