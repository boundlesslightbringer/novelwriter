# Pacing Examples

## Input/Output Example

### Input

```yaml
Module 1.1 excerpt (Caradhen of the Thornback):

  Psychological/behavioral baseline:
    Habitual defense: encodes emotion in what he observes rather than naming
      it — displaces interior state onto external detail.
    Interiority: moderately high, but organised and purposeful.
    Cognitive style: sequential and tactical.
    Under pressure: goes still and very precise. Does not accelerate.

  Current state:
    Emotional state: The brittle calm of a decision already made but not
      yet enacted. His chest is tight. Awake since before the courier arrived.
    Active desire: To act before he thinks too carefully and stops himself.
    Active fear: That thinking will become an obstacle.
    Arc stage: Crisis approach.
    Physical state: Fatigued. Has not eaten.

Most recent scene outcome:
  Caradhen received word that Vara's assessment has reached Aldric and the
  contingency has been activated. He has one day to act.

Chapter emotional arc:
  Opens in administrative normalcy. Moves through the arrival of the news,
  through the decision, to a close that is committed action.
```

### Output

```yaml
Character Psychology Summary:
  Caradhen's habitual response to pressure is tactical stillness — he goes
  precise, not fast. Under acute pressure, his defense compresses further:
  sentences shorten, political observation disappears, and he begins counting
  things. His current state is at the outer edge of what this defense can hold.

Scene Rhythm Register: BRITTLE.

  Caradhen's psychology does not accelerate under pressure — it stills and
  compresses. Urgent would be wrong for this character even in a scene of
  genuine crisis. He is not genuinely in control — he is performing control
  over a decision that is costing him more than he is admitting. Brittle makes
  this character's specific crisis legible — the managed fracture of a man
  whose management system is working against him.

  Register shift within scene: Brief shift to Taut at the moment of physical
  movement toward the decision.

Sentence Density: HIGH.
  Caradhen's cognitive style is sequential and tactical — each observation
  carries an implication. His shoulder ache is available as physical interruption.

Interiority Level: COMPRESSED.
  His baseline interiority is moderate-high but he is actively suppressing
  it. Interior passages should be short and self-interrupting.

Dialogue-to-Action Ratio: ACTION-HEAVY / DIALOGUE-SPARSE.
  Caradhen goes silent under genuine internal pressure.

Chapter Arc Alignment:
  This scene is the crisis point. The pacing should tighten incrementally
  toward the moment of action. The chapter's final beat is movement.

Tension Flags:
  — UNDERCUT RISK: Caradhen's displacement habit is so established that if
    rendered cleanly, the scene will be emotionally flat. The displacement
    must show its seams — one moment where the analytical observation almost
    names what it is encoding, then cuts back.
  — VOICE SPECIFICITY RISK: Under maximum compression his voice risks losing
    the political observation. Markers to protect: (1) he counts things;
    (2) he reads power geometry in rooms even when alone.
```

## Quality Checklist

Before returning pacing guidance, verify:

- [ ] Character Psychology Summary is specific — could not apply to a different character.
- [ ] Register is derived from character psychology intersected with current state.
- [ ] Register justification traces the derivation explicitly.
- [ ] All output sections are present: psychology summary, register + justification, sentence density, interiority level, dialogue ratio, chapter arc alignment, tension flags.
- [ ] Tension flags are character-specific — named behavioral patterns.
- [ ] Register assignment checked: would a different character in the same situation land in a different register?
- [ ] No prose written. No plot decisions. No scene content specified.
- [ ] No World Bible writes specified.
