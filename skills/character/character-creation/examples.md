## Example

### Input shape

```
Character concept document:
  Role: Regional warlord — obstacle and potential reluctant ally.
  Core contradiction: Has kept his people alive for thirty years by never fighting a battle he could avoid / believes a man who will not fight for what is sacred deserves nothing he has.
  Sketch: [one-paragraph prose sketch]
  Open questions:
    1. What event taught him that commitment leads to catastrophe?
    2. What does he believe about himself that is completely untrue?
    3. Is there something for which he would break his own rule?
    4. How does he treat people who show the open courage he denies himself?
    5. What will the protagonist offer that breaks him open?
```

### Output shape (abbreviated)

```
MODULE 1.1 RECORD — Caradhen of the Thornback

Identity: [Full physical description, what people notice, what they miss]
Social position: Clan-Lord of the Thornback passes. Reputation: untrustworthy opportunist (lowland lords). Reality: tactical survivor who has never broken a promise he actually made.

Core wound: At age twenty-two, led his father's warband into an alliance battle on the western plains. The alliance collapsed. Half his warband died in an afternoon. He concluded: other people's causes are pits without bottoms. He has been correct often enough that he has never had to re-examine the conclusion.

Core desire: To hand something intact to the next generation — passes, people, customs — without having lost them in someone else's war.

Core fear: That the thing he is protecting has already been hollowed out — that the people he is preserving no longer remember what they are for.

Self-deception: Believes he is protecting his people. Is also protecting himself from the grief of the plains, which he has never allowed to fully arrive.

Values under pressure:
  [detailed specification]

Response patterns:
  Betrayal: Goes quiet. Stops trusting for reasons. Never explicitly confronts; simply removes access.
  Loss: Absorbs. Continues. Privately catastrophic; publicly unchanged. The grief has nowhere to go.
  [remaining patterns]

Arc type: Revelation. He does not change — he is revealed. What looked like pragmatism is exposed as grief with a policy.
Arc stage at story start: Established. The audience and the protagonist will read him as political; the arc is the progressive stripping of that reading.

MODULE 1.2 INITIAL ENTRIES — [knowledge entries]

MODULE 1.3 INITIAL ENTRIES — [pre-story relationship records]
```
