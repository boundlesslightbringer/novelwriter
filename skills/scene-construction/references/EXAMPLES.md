# Scene Construction Examples

## Input/Output Example

### Input

```yaml
Scene parameters (from Gardener Plotting System):
  POV: Caradhen of the Thornback
  Goal: Conclude the envoy's follow-up meeting before Vara can move the
        conversation toward the question she has been building toward.
  Obstacle: Vara shifts the conversation without warning — she stops asking
        about trade routes and asks, with apparent casualness, which of
        Caradhen's sons he expects to outlive him. The question is not about
        his sons. Internal obstacle: Caradhen's survival pattern is to
        non-commit and exit. The question has trapped him where exiting
        would itself be a signal.
  Choice: Answer the question — which means engaging on terms Vara has set
        and revealing something about succession and loyalty — or refuse it,
        which means making visible that the question was not casual.
  Consequence shape: Whichever option he takes, Vara has what she came for:
        a data point about how Caradhen manages the question of legacy.

Module 1.1 excerpt: [emotional state: controlled surface / something older and
  colder moving beneath; observational focus: power geometry of rooms, exits,
  sightlines, what people are doing with their hands; processing style: encodes
  emotion in what he notices, never names it directly; voice: long deliberate
  sentences in control, compresses toward declaratives under pressure]

Module 1.2 excerpt: Caradhen DOES NOT KNOW Vara's full assessment brief.
  SUSPECTS she has more authority than her opening position suggests.
  KNOWS the question about his sons is not casual.

Module 2.4 excerpt: Payoff 003 — Caradhen's thirty-year survival pattern
  (Foreshadowing). Window: open. Status: Pending.
  [The scene is a natural opportunity to advance this element — his
  survival pattern is being directly tested by Vara's question.]

Pacing guidance: TAUT. Compressed interiority. High sentence density.
  Dialogue-sparse relative to action. Voice flag: protect observational
  precision — he notices what she is doing with her hands.
```

### Output

```markdown
The solar had one door and three windows, and Vara had seated herself where
she could see all of them.

Caradhen had noticed this when she arrived. He had not remarked on it.

They had been discussing the northern pass for twenty minutes — road
conditions, the weight limits of supply carts, the particular problem of
the second switchback in early spring. Vara listened the way she always
listened: with her full attention and no notes. He was explaining the
drainage issue on the third terrace when she said, with the specific quality
of casualness that was not casual at all:

"Which of your sons do you expect to outlive you?"

The window above her right shoulder showed a strip of grey sky. He looked at
it for a moment.

Not long. But a moment.

She did not fill the silence. She never filled silences; that was one of
the things he had catalogued about her in the past four days. She placed
silences and waited to see what grew in them.

He had three sons. He had thoughts about each of them that he had not
organised into anything he would call expectations. The question assumed
he thought about his own death in terms of succession. He thought about
his own death in terms of who would know, when it happened, that it had
happened, and how quickly.

"Davin manages the eastern passes," he said. "He has the patience for it."

He watched her right hand.

Her right hand did not move.

"That isn't what I asked," she said, pleasantly.

"No," he said. "It isn't."

He looked back at the strip of grey sky. Somewhere in the account she was
building — the assessment brief he did not know existed but whose shape he
had begun to understand — there was now a data point. He had answered by
not answering, and she had noted what he had chosen instead.

The drainage issue on the third terrace, he thought, remained unresolved.
```

## Quality Checklist

Before returning the draft, verify:

- [ ] Every sentence passes the POV discipline test: only what this character can perceive, know, and interpret from their position.
- [ ] No head-hopping, no omniscient intrusions, no external self-description, no knowledge violations.
- [ ] Voice profile is active from the first sentence and sustained throughout.
- [ ] Scene has all three phases: opening (character in situation), middle (obstacle encountered, choice live), ending (consequence opening, scene closed on a specific beat).
- [ ] Foreshadowing, if embedded, passes the organic integration test.
- [ ] Pacing guidance, if provided, is respected.
- [ ] Any parameter discrepancy discovered during writing is flagged for the main agent.
- [ ] No prose quality refinement attempted — draft quality only.
- [ ] No World Bible writes specified.
