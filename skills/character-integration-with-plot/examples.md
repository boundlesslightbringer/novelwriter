## Example

### Input shape

```
Characters for integration: Caradhen (warlord), Vara (protagonist's envoy), Sethric (Caradhen's second-in-command).

Module 1.1 excerpts: [full psychological records for all three]
Module 1.2 initial entries: [from Character Creation for each]
Module 1.3 initial entries: [Caradhen knows Sethric since childhood; Vara has no prior relationship with either]
Module 3.1 excerpt: [three-faction political map; Thornback passes as strategic neutral zone; protagonist's faction has recently seized lowland cities]
```

### Output shape (abbreviated)

```
MODULE 1.2 EXPANSION

Caradhen:
  KNOWS: Sethric's father died in the plains battle (causal not coincidental — Caradhen's decision); Sethric does not know Caradhen knows he knows (Sethric believes Caradhen thinks he has absolved himself). Vara is an envoy; has been sent because the protagonist cannot come personally — which means either the protagonist is occupied or does not want to show personal investment. Caradhen suspects the latter.
  SUSPECTS: Vara has authority to offer more than her initial position. The visit is exploratory, not final.
  BELIEVES (possibly false): Believes Sethric has forgiven him. Sethric has not.
  DOES NOT KNOW: Vara is not primarily a diplomat — she is also carrying an assessment brief for her principal. She is evaluating whether Caradhen is an asset or an obstacle to be removed.

[Vara and Sethric knowledge matrices follow]

MODULE 1.3 FULL RECORDS

Caradhen → Sethric:
  Valence: Complex. Genuine trust in Sethric's competence and loyalty; persistent low-level guilt about the plains battle that has never been spoken aloud; belief that the guilt has been absorbed and managed.
  Power: Caradhen holds formal power; Sethric holds informal moral authority that Caradhen would never acknowledge.
  History: Grew up together; Sethric's father served Caradhen's father. The plains battle is the load-bearing moment — Sethric was there, survived, and has never mentioned it.
  Secrets: Caradhen conceals that he thinks about it often. Sethric conceals that he has never stopped thinking about it.
  Trajectory: Stable surface; pressure fault underneath. A moment of direct challenge to Caradhen's authority — especially a moment requiring commitment — may surface the unspoken structure.

Sethric → Caradhen:
  Valence: Complex in the opposite direction. Loyalty that coexists with a grief that has calcified into a judgment he has never delivered.
  Power: Sethric holds none formally; holds the specific power of a witness — he could break Caradhen's self-narrative at any moment by speaking.
  [continues]

[Vara records follow]

MODULE 4.1 INITIAL ENTRIES

Caradhen — desire loop:
  Driver: Preserve the passes and the people intact; avoid commitment that costs what he protects.
  Anticipated action pattern: Will offer apparent cooperation without actual commitment; will read every proposal for its commitment cost before anything else.
  Anticipated immediate consequence: The protagonist's envoy (Vara) will read his non-commitment as stalling or as receptivity, depending on how sophisticated her brief is.
  Affected characters: Vara (immediate), the protagonist (delayed), Sethric (if Caradhen is eventually forced to commit, Sethric's judgment becomes load-bearing).
  Time-delayed consequence potential: YES — if Caradhen makes a promise to Vara that he does not intend to keep, and Vara's assessment brief reaches the protagonist, the discovery that Caradhen was managing them rather than negotiating with them may produce a response that forces the very confrontation he has spent thirty years avoiding.
```
